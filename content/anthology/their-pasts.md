---
title: Their Pasts
authors:
  - Ama Codjoe
---

When you are a woman attracted to men, you may

fall in love with a person who promised he’d return

to his lover’s hospital bed, but never did;

was advised by his father: Your first screw

should have nothing to do with love; had a mother

who, in one way or another, could not nurse him;

swore on something holy, a scripture, his grandmother’s

grave, that he wouldn’t do it again; has cheated

and been cheated on; used his knee

to open a woman’s legs and took the absence of no

as an asking, a yes; said they could have

other children just not this one

and dumped her after the abortion; wants

to slaughter the other team; is addicted to being

on top; is addicted to getting high; learned to harden

heartbreak into spikes; was taught to hurt himself

last and others first, as if it were possible

to bruise without getting bruised;

remembers the shame that poured

from inside him like sweat staining the pits

of a white undershirt; who, like you, bears a wound

that, after all this time, carries on with its weeping;

who likes the sound of the word bully,

not what it means, but its music; who rings

your doorbell and carries behind his back

a bouquet of tiger lilies, all but one closed tightly.
