title: "If Strangers meet"
authors:
  - "E.E.Cummings"

If strangers meet  

life begins-  

not poor not rich  

(only aware)  

kind neither  

nor cruel  

(only complete)  

i not not you  

not possible;  

only truthful  

-truthfully, once  

if strangers(who  

deep our most are  

selves)touch:  

forever
